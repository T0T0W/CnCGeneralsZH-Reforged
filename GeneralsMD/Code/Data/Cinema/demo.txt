# A tour of the verbs, written for the parade scenario:
#
#   generals.exe -win -xres 1280 -yres 720 -quickstart -noshellmap -observer -multiInstance
#     -randommap 1 2 small -autoskirmish 2 -takeover -seed 1 -peacetime 10
#     -scenario parade -cinema demo
#
# Add -video 0 1200 demo to get Videos\demo.mp4 instead of watching it (ffmpeg on the PATH).
# Coordinates are for that map: the column forms up round (700,550) and (1000,250), the order goes
# out at 3 seconds and sends it to (1000,1150).
#
# <seconds> <verb> ...
#   cut <x> <y> [zoom] [angle] [pitch]   be there now
#   move <x> <y> <s>                      slide to a point
#   route <s> <x> <y> <x> <y> ...        slide through points on a curve
#   zoom <factor> <s>                     1 is the distance the list started at, below 1 is closer
#   angle <deg> <s> / orbit <deg> <s>     turn to a heading / turn by this much
#   pitch <deg> <s>                       tilt, -36 to 36; 0 is the game's own
#   follow <template> [s]                 chase the nearest one of those
#   unfollow | hud on|off | letterbox on|off | shot | end

0     cut        850 420 1.1 0 0
0     letterbox  on
# come down over the column and swing round it while it sets off
1     zoom       0.6 5
1     pitch      -20 5
1     orbit      60 8
# ride with one tank
4     follow     AmericaTankCrusader 12
5     zoom       0.4 4
# pull back and fly ahead of them to where they are going, then circle it as they arrive
16    route      8 900 950 1000 1150
16    zoom       1.0 6
16    pitch      0 6
22    orbit      -150 12
28    shot
30    zoom       0.55 6
37    shot
38    end
